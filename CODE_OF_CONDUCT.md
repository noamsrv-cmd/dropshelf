# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DropShelf a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and focusing on what is best for users and the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy, and take proportionate corrective action.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
